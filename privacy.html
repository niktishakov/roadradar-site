Road Radar
Phuket road events from riders

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Road Radar shows road events in Phuket (police checks, accidents, floods, hazards) reported by other riders. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Location

The app asks for location access only while it is open (“While Using the App”). It never tracks you in the background. Your location is used on your device to show events ahead of you and to place a report where you are. When you send a report or answer “Still there?”, the app sends that point, your direction of travel and GPS accuracy to our server.

Account

You can use the map without an account. To send reports you sign in with Apple or Google. We store an account identifier and the email address the provider shares (Apple may give a private relay address). If you sign in with Google, Google also shares your name and profile photo link; they are stored with your account and shown only to you. A name you enter yourself is stored only on your device.

What other riders see

Other riders see events and a “N participants” count. They never see your name, email or account.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps, no selling or sharing of data with third parties. The app has no analytics SDKs.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database hosting) in Singapore. Events expire automatically after a short time set per event type.

Deleting your account

Open Account → Delete account in the app. Your account, sign-in and personal stats are deleted. Reports you already sent stay on the map without any link to you until they expire.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
